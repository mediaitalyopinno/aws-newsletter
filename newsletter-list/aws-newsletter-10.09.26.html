Surge Acceleration Team — September 2026
The EMEA GTM Surge Acceleration team helps Surge and selected EMEA customers accelerate GenAI use cases from opportunity to production. We provide LoB CxOs with actionable, industry-specific recommendations and connect them with the right AWS expertise, partners and ISVs.
Across Healthcare & Life Sciences, Travel & Hospitality, Media, Entertainment, Games & Sports, and ISVs, one signal is clear: customers are moving beyond GenAI experimentation toward agentic systems, semantic data foundations and enterprise-scale AI governance.
 
The trends we’re spotting in the market
 
 
 
Healthcare & Life Sciences
•
Semantic Data Foundations & Agentic AI Systems
Life sciences organizations are prioritizing ontology and semantic data layers as the foundation for agentic AI. Norstella is connecting preclinical, clinical, commercial and payer ontologies, while Boehringer Ingelheim, Chiesi and ICON are advancing agentic workflows across research, regulatory and clinical operations.
AWS angle: Norstella aligned with AWS on an end-to-end ontology connecting the Molecular Index Code with clinical, commercial and payer data.
 
•
Trusted Research Environments & Governed Health Data Platforms
Healthcare is converging on governed platforms for secure, multi-stakeholder data access. KAUST is implementing Trusted Research Environments, Oura is developing Longitudinal Health Data Platforms, and Topicus Healthcare is participating in Dutch national health data exchange pilots. These models combine consent, provenance and compliance, reflecting the Seven Safes framework for patient confidentiality at national scale.
 
 
 
Travel & Hospitality
•
Travel Industry Shifts from GenAI Experimentation to Agentic Production with Pricing and Cost Control Challenges
Travel is moving from GenAI experimentation toward operational AI deployment. easyJet is migrating 400 users to Claude on Amazon Bedrock, Amadeus is scaling Kiro from 1.5K toward 5K users, and Google launched agentic hotel booking with Expedia, Booking.com, Hilton and Marriott.
AWS angle: easyJet is moving to Bedrock for sovereignty, while Amadeus received $200K in AWS credits to address pricing concerns.
 
•
Enterprise Cost Control as the Production Unlock
Cost visibility is becoming critical as enterprise AI scales. Amadeus scaled Kiro after GitHub Copilot pricing increased 3–4x, while easyJet is facing cost attribution and IAM challenges as it migrates 400 users to Claude on Bedrock.
AWS angle: Amazon Bedrock introduced principal-based attribution, Projects and per-request tagging to support production-scale AI deployments.
 
 
 
Media, Entertainment,
Games & Sports
•
CEE Gaming Consolidates Around Live-Service Models + Practical AI
CEE gaming studios are consolidating around evergreen live-service titles while adopting practical AI. Playrix runs GPU-intensive GenAI on H100 for visual-asset generation and migrated engineering from Cursor to Claude Code on Bedrock. Wargaming increased Claude spend 45x in two months, from $2,300 to $104,000 MRR.
AWS angle: Bedrock cost attribution enabled broader developer access at Wargaming, while Playrix moved engineering-wide to Claude Code on Bedrock.
 
•
EMEA Publishing Under AI-Driven Traffic Collapse
Generative AI is disrupting publisher traffic: users click traditional links in only 8% of searches with AI summaries versus 15% without. Traffic via OpenAI is 750x harder and Anthropic 30,000x harder, increasing pressure on publishers to rethink licensing, content protection and newsroom AI.
AWS angle: AWS BD is qualifying three use cases: archive licensing/RAG, WAF Bot Control + C2PA provenance, and assistive newsroom AI.
  
 
 
Independent Software
Vendors
•
Multi-Agent AI Architectures Gain Enterprise Traction
Customers are moving beyond single-model inference toward multi-agent orchestration. Spotnana launched specialized agents for corporate travel, dsm-firmenich is prioritizing co-scientist orchestration with shared multi-agent frameworks, and EMEA HCLS accounts are exploring autonomous research workbenches and clinical trial automation. Research shows architectural choices can create 100x latency differences and coordination success rates ranging from above 90% to below 30%.
 
•
Platform Consolidation & Unified AI Governance
Enterprises face common challenges around IAM federation, cost attribution, MCP complexity and skills as they consolidate AI access. Wargaming, easyJet and Playrix show the same pattern: customers are seeking unified AI governance through a single platform, with cost visibility becoming critical for adoption at scale.
AWS angle: AAmazon introduced principal-based cost attribution, application-level Projects and per-request metadata tagging on Bedrock.
